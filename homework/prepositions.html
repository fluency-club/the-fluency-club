Lesson Recap — Past Perfect Continuous ⏳

had + been + verb-ing

Use it for an activity that was happening for some time before another past moment.

She had been waiting for 40 minutes when the bus arrived.

Cause / evidence

Use it when an earlier activity explains a past result.

His shoes were wet because he had been walking in the rain.

Past Perfect vs Past Perfect Continuous:
had + V3 → completed result / amount. She had written three pages.
had been + -ing → activity / duration / cause. She had been writing for three hours.
