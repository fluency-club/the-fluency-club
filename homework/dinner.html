Useful Restaurant Phrases

OrderingCould we start with the…? · I’ll have the…, please.
RecommendationWhat would you recommend if I’m looking for something…?
ModificationWould it be possible to leave out the…?
Polite complaintI’m sorry, but I think this may be undercooked.
Checking a problemWould you mind checking this for me?
Sending food backCould this be sent back to the kitchen?
Service chargeIs the service charge already included?
FinishingCould we have the bill/check when you get a chance?

Passive Voice

be + V3. Focus on the food, service, process or result: The bread is baked in-house. We were offered dessert. Our table is being prepared.

Past tenses

Past Simple: finished event / sequence / storyline.
Past Continuous: action in progress / background scene.
