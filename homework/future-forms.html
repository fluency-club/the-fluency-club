Future Forms: Meaning, Not Memorising 🚀

Review Future Continuous and compare it with will, be going to, and Present Continuous for future arrangements. Finish with a short email and a speaking recording.

Lesson Summary 🧠

Future Simple — will + verb

Predictions, spontaneous decisions, promises, offers and future facts.

I think she’ll accept. · I’ll call him now.

be going to + verb

A prior intention, or a prediction based on evidence you can see now.

I’m going to change jobs. · It’s going to rain.

Present Continuous — future

A personal arrangement that is already organised: person, time, place, booking or calendar commitment.

I’m meeting Nina at 7 tomorrow.

Future Continuous

Move mentally to a future moment and describe an action already in progress, or part of the expected course of events.

At 7 tomorrow, I’ll be meeting Nina.

The key contrast

WILL = prediction / decisionGOING TO = prior intention / evidencePRESENT CONTINUOUS = fixed arrangementFUTURE CONTINUOUS = action underway at a future point
