Present tenses

Present Simple — routine/fact
Present Continuous — now/temporary
Present Perfect — experience/result
Present Perfect Continuous — activity/duration

Past Simple vs Past Continuous

Past Simple: finished event / sequence.
Past Continuous: action in progress / background scene.
