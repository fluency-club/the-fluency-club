Today’s Grammar Map 🧠

The tense is chosen by meaning, not by one magic signal word.

Present Simple

base verb / -s

Use: routines, facts, repeated actions, schedules, and states.

I work remotely twice a week.
The class starts at 6.

Present Continuous

am / is / are + -ing

Use: now, temporary situations, trends, changes, and personal arrangements.

I’m working from a café this week.
I’m meeting a client tomorrow.

Present Perfect

have / has + V3

Use: experience, recent result, unfinished time, and past actions connected to now.

I’ve finished three projects this month.
I’ve already sent the email.
📸 STATE = snapshot

A state is simply true: opinion, knowledge, possession, preference, understanding.

I know. · I believe. · I own. · I prefer.

🎥 ACTION = video

An activity, process, temporary behavior, or change can be imagined unfolding.

I’m working. · I’m changing. · I’m considering.

think
I think it’s useful = opinion/state.
I’m thinking about it = considering/action.
have
I have two brothers = possession/relationship.
I’m having lunch = activity.
see
I see your point = understand/state.
I’m seeing a client = meeting/arrangement.
State from past → now: use Present Perfect Simple: I’ve known her for years. We do not normally say I’ve been knowing her... because know describes a state.

6. Reading — The Routine Experiment 📖

Leo usually starts his day with the most demanding task because he knows his concentration is strongest in the morning. This month, however, he is testing a different schedule. He is taking a longer lunch break and is turning off notifications for two hours every afternoon. So far, the experiment has worked surprisingly well: he has streamlined several repetitive tasks and has become more consistent with his priorities. He still has a heavy workload, but he feels less overwhelmed because the new routine is more sustainable.
