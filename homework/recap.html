4. Required Audio 🎙️

60–90 seconds · use your real life

Speak naturally about your actual week or recent life. You do not need to invent anything.

  • something you usually do → Present Simple
  • something temporary or different this week → Present Continuous
  • something you have accomplished recently → Present Perfect
  • something you have been doing lately → Present Perfect Continuous
  • one real recent event → Past Simple
  • what was happening at one moment → Past Continuous
  • something that had happened before another past event → Past Perfect
  • something that had been going on before a past moment → Past Perfect Continuous
  • use at least two of: through · throughout · thorough · though · thought
