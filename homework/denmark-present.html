Lesson Recap 🧠

Present Simple

base verb / he-she-it + -s

Routine, fact, state, repeated pattern.

The narrator usually hides one clue in each chapter.

usuallyoftenevery

Present Continuous

am / is / are + -ing

Now, temporary situation, change around now.

The detective is checking the footage now.

nowthis weekcurrently

Present Perfect

have / has + V3

Result, experience, completed quantity, change up to now.

The police have found three fingerprints so far.

so faralreadyyet

Present Perfect Continuous

have / has been + -ing

Duration, repeated recent activity, process continuing to now.

They have been questioning witnesses all morning.

forsinceall morning
Result / number → Present Perfect. Activity / duration → Present Perfect Continuous.

5. Reading — The Second Missing Book 📖🕵️

A university library normally keeps its rarest books in a locked room that only two librarians can enter. This month, however, the library is running a special exhibition, so more staff members are working near the collection than usual.

One first edition has disappeared, but nobody knows exactly when. Security officers have reviewed six hours of camera footage so far and have found one unusual detail: a researcher appears near the locked room three times. The researcher says he has an alibi. He has been working on a biography of the author for nearly two years, and he claims he only visits the archive to check references.

The head librarian has been questioning staff members since early morning. She usually trusts her team, but today she is treating everyone as a possible suspect. Meanwhile, readers online have been discussing the missing book all day, and several people have already invented dramatic theories about the motive.

6. Short Writing — Case Update ✍️

Write 120–160 words. Give a live update on the missing-book investigation.

Use all four present tenses and at least five mystery words. Include: the normal routine, what is happening now, what investigators have discovered, and what they have been doing.
