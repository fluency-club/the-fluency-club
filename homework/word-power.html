-ive create → creative

-ous danger → dangerous

-ly clear → clearly

⚠ Common traps
  • Don’t invent a form just because a suffix “looks right”: decidement → decision.
  • Spelling can change: happy → happily, possible → possibility.
  • Not every family has all four forms. Learn the forms you actually meet.
  • After be / seem / become, you usually need an adjective — not an adverb.
3-second rule: What part of speech do I need? → Which family do I know? → Which suffix/prefix fits? → Does the final sentence sound natural?
