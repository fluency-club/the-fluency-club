Future Status Check ✈️

Quick review of today’s lesson: will, be going to, and Present Continuous for future arrangements — plus the planning vocabulary from class.

Approx. 15–20 minutes.

Lesson recap — three ways to talk about the future

The event can be similar. What changes is the status of the plan and the speaker’s meaning.

WILL

will + base verb

Decision now: “That sounds good — I’ll come.”

Opinion prediction: “I think the trip will be amazing.”

Offer / promise: “I’ll help you.”

BE GOING TO

am/is/are going to + verb

Prior intention: “I’m going to apply next week.”

Visible evidence: “Look at those clouds — it’s going to rain.”

The decision existed before the moment of speaking.

PRESENT CONTINUOUS

am/is/are + verb-ing + future time

Confirmed arrangement: “I’m meeting Laura at 7 tomorrow.”

Usually there is external confirmation: a time, place, booking, ticket, appointment or agreement.

Fast contrast:
“I’ll meet Alex.” = decision / offer now.
“I’m going to meet Alex.” = intention already in my mind.
“I’m meeting Alex at 6 at Café Luna.” = arranged and concrete.

1. Future Signal Scanner

Choose the form that best matches the meaning. More than one form may sometimes be grammatically possible, but one is the most natural for the signal given.

2. Same future, different meaning

Focus on what the speaker is communicating — not only the grammar shape.
