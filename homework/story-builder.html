How to save your answers

  1. Work in your normal browser. Your choices and typed answers save automatically on this device. Check the save message below.
  2. Taking a break? Press Save answers now, then Download answers backup. Keep the small .json file. You can split this homework into two sittings.
  3. Coming back? Open this same homework in the same browser and press Resume answers saved in this browser below. This button is always visible. The page opens blank until you choose to resume. If there is no draft on this device, the button explains what to do.
  4. Changing device or browser? Press Upload answers backup (.json) below and choose your saved .json file. Private/incognito mode and clearing browser data can remove your local draft.
  5. Keep the recording too. Audio is not part of the browser answers draft. Download your clip before leaving, then upload it when you return. It is sent together with the PDF when you press Send to Teacher.

Saving is not sending. When everything is ready, use Send to Teacher at the bottom and wait for server confirmation.

Our last lesson · quick reminder

Word formation: first find the word class: noun, adjective, adverb, or verb. Then check the meaning, prefix, spelling, and any plural or verb ending.

Past Simple: finished event / sequence. Past Continuous: activity underway then. Past Perfect: an earlier completed result. Past Perfect Continuous: had been + -ing, an earlier process or duration. State meanings such as know normally use a simple form.
