🧠 Lesson recap — Grammar

Past Perfect

had + V3

An action completed before another past action/moment.

By the time we arrived, the plane had left.

Present Perfect

have / has + V3

Past connected to now: experience, present result, unfinished time.

I've visited New York twice.

Present Perfect Continuous

have / has + been + verb-ing

Use it for an activity that started in the past and continues now, or a recent/repeated activity with a present result.

I've been driving for three hours.

Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous

Present Perfect → result, completion, amount: I've visited five states.

Present Perfect Continuous → process, duration, repetition: I've been travelling for two weeks.

Quick self-check ✨

Can you explain the difference without notes?

  • Past Perfect: what happened before a past moment.
  • Present Perfect: what happened in the past but is connected to now.
  • Present Perfect Continuous: what has been happening — focus on process or duration.
