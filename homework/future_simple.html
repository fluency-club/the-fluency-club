Future Simple Recap — will / won’t ⚡

Positive
subject + will + base verb

I will call. She will understand.

Negative
subject + won’t + base verb

It won’t take long. We won’t forget.

Question
Will + subject + base verb?

Will you send it? What will happen?

1. Prediction / opinion

I think the interview will go well. Prices probably won’t stay this low.

2. Instant decision

The phone is ringing — I’ll get it.

3. Promise / commitment

Don’t worry. I won’t tell anyone.

4. Offer / willingness / request / refusal

I’ll carry that for you. Will you send me the link? I won’t sign under those conditions.

Important: after future time words such as when, as soon as, before, after, until, if, English normally uses a present form, not will: As soon as I know, I’ll tell you. Also remember: will is not the automatic tense for every future plan.
