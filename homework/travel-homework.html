3. Present Perfect Focus 🌍

Form: have/has + past participle.
Use it for: life experience, a recent result, or an action connected to now.
Common words: already, just, yet, ever, never, for, since.
Examples: I have already checked in. She has just found her gate. Have you ever missed a flight? We haven’t boarded yet.

4. Choose the Right Tense ⏰

Present Simple: routines and facts — “I usually fly economy.”
Present Continuous: happening now or temporary — “I’m waiting at Gate 6.”
Present Perfect: past action connected to now — “They have changed the gate.”
