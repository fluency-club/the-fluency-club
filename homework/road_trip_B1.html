🧠 Lesson recap — Grammar

Past Perfect

had + V3

An action completed before another past action/moment.

By the time we arrived, the plane had left.

Present Perfect

have / has + V3

Past connected to now: experience, present result, unfinished time.

I've visited New York twice.
