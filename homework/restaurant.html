Lesson recap — Present tenses 🧭

Present Simple

base verb / verb + s

Routine or preference: I usually order soup first.

Present Continuous

am/is/are + -ing

Now or temporary: The server is bringing our drinks.

Present Perfect

have/has + V3

Experience or result: We’ve already ordered dessert.

Present Perfect Continuous

have/has been + -ing

Ongoing activity: We’ve been waiting for twenty minutes.

Key contrast: Present Perfect focuses on a result or completed experience; Present Perfect Continuous emphasizes the activity and duration.
