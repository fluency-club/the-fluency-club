Future Impact

A short recap, 12 choices, and five one-sentence transformations.

1 · A quick recap

Future Perfect: completed by then

will have + past participle
By Friday, we’ll have wrapped up the review.
Compare: At 3, we’ll be reviewing it = in progress.
Time clause: By the time it goes live, we’ll have tested it.

Future forms & deadlines

will: prediction or decision now.
going to: earlier intention or present evidence.
Present Continuous: arrangement.
Future Continuous: activity in progress / expected plans.

Lesson focus: project milestones, staying on track, and completing work before a deadline. “He’ll have gone by now” can also express a confident assumption about a completed event.

2 · Eight useful expressions

milestone

важный этап / контрольная точка

By June, we’ll have reached the first major milestone.

on track

идти по плану

We’re on track to finish before the deadline.

ahead of schedule

с опережением графика

They completed the prototype ahead of schedule.

fall behind

отставать от графика

If we lose another week, we’ll fall behind.

wrap up

завершить / закончить

We should wrap up the final review by Thursday.

iron out

уладить / устранить мелкие проблемы

By launch day, we’ll have ironed out the main issues.

roll out

запустить / внедрить

The company will roll out the new system next quarter.

stakeholder

заинтересованная сторона / участник проекта

All key stakeholders need to approve the final version.
