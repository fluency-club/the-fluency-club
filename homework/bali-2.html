🧠 Passive Voice — Complete Cheat Sheet

Core idea: the object of the active sentence becomes the subject of the passive sentence. The tense lives in BE; the main verb stays in the past participle (V3).

Active: The hotel renovated the rooms. → Passive: The rooms were renovated.
TensePassive formExampleUse / note
Present Simpleam / is / are + V3The rooms are cleaned daily.Very common.
Present Continuousam / is / are being + V3The pool is being repaired.Action in progress now.
Present Perfecthave / has been + V3The lobby has been renovated.Completed result connected to now.
Present Perfect Continuoushave / has been being + V3The road has been being repaired for weeks.Grammatically possible but awkward. Usually rewrite in active voice.
Past Simplewas / were + V3The temple was built centuries ago.Very common.
Past Continuouswas / were being + V3The road was being repaired.Past action in progress.
Past Perfecthad been + V3The itinerary had been changed.Completed before another past event.
Past Perfect Continuoushad been being + V3The hotel had been being renovated.Possible but very rare; normally rewrite.
Future Simplewill be + V3The room will be prepared by 3.Very common.
Future Continuouswill be being + V3The road will be being repaired.Possible but uncommon; often rewrite.
Future Perfectwill have been + V3The work will have been completed by June.Useful for deadlines/results.
Future Perfect Continuouswill have been being + V3The resort will have been being renovated for a year.Theoretical and extremely rare. Avoid in normal English.

Modal verbs

modal + be + V3

must be respected · should be protected · can be booked · might be cancelled

Perfect modals

modal + have been + V3

should have been informed · might have been delayed · could have been avoided

Going to

am/is/are going to be + V3

The pool is going to be renovated.

Passive infinitive

to be + V3

The work is expected to be completed soon.

Passive gerund

being + V3

I dislike being photographed.

Perfect passive gerund

having been + V3

She denied having been told earlier.

Important passive patterns

PatternRuleExample
Agent with byAdd by + doer only when the agent matters.The resort was designed by a local architect.
Instrument with withUse with for the tool/material, not the doer.The room was decorated with flowers.
Two objectsEither object can sometimes become the subject; the person is often more natural.We were offered an upgrade. / An upgrade was offered to us.
Reporting passiveCommon in formal English: It is said that… / He is believed to…Bali is believed to attract millions of visitors.
Prepositional verbsKeep the preposition.The problem was dealt with quickly.
Get-passiveget + V3 is more informal and often describes an event/change.Our flight got cancelled.
Have/get something doneCausative structure: someone else performs the service for you.We had our room upgraded.
When passive is impossible or usually a bad choice:
  • Most intransitive verbs have no object, so they cannot normally become passive: arrive, happen, occur, sleep, die, exist, appear.
  • Do not force rare continuous-perfect passives just because the form exists. Natural English usually prefers an active rewrite.
  • Use active voice when the doer is the main point.

📘 Articles — Complete Cheat Sheet

A / AN

One singular countable noun when it is non-specific, first mentioned, or one example of a group.

a hotel · an island · she is a photographer

THE

Specific/identified nouns, shared knowledge, unique things, superlatives, ordinals, and many geographical groups.

the hotel we booked · the best beach

— ZERO ARTICLE

General plural/uncountable nouns, most names, languages, sports, meals, subjects, and many fixed expressions.

Tourists love — Bali. We had — breakfast.

Use A / AN with…Examples
one non-specific singular countable nouna villa · an excursion
jobs / roles / identityShe is a guide. He is an architect.
meaning “one / any”Can I have a towel?
rates / frequencytwice a week · $100 a night
exclamations with singular nounsWhat a view!
Use THE with…Examples
something already mentioned or clearly identifiedI booked a villa. The villa has a pool.
unique things / shared contextthe sun · the internet · the reception desk
superlatives and ordinal numbersthe best · the most beautiful · the first · the 21st
adjectives representing groups of peoplethe rich · the elderly
musical instruments in general skillplay the piano
rivers, seas, oceans, deserts, mountain rangesthe Nile · the Pacific · the Sahara · the Alps
groups of islands / plural country namesthe Maldives · the Netherlands · the United States
hotels, cinemas, theatres, museums, many descriptive building namesthe Ritz · the British Museum
families as a groupthe Smiths
Use ZERO ARTICLE with…Examples
plural countable nouns in generalHotels can be expensive.
uncountable nouns in generalTourism creates jobs. Nature matters.
most countries, cities, streets, single mountains/lakes/islandsBali · Indonesia · London · Mount Batur · Lake Como
languages, school subjects, sportsEnglish · history · tennis
meals when speaking generallyhave breakfast · after dinner
days, months, most holidaysMonday · September · Christmas
transport after byby car · by plane · by train
home / work / bed / school / university / prison / hospital when used for their normal purposego to bed · be at work · go to school
Institution contrast: She is in hospital (BrE: as a patient) vs She is in the hospital (a specific hospital/building; AmE usually uses the hospital in both senses).

🔢 Articles with numbers — the part people forget

Number patternRuleExample
Ordinal numbersUsually use the before first, second, third, next in a fixed sequence, etc.the first day · the second hotel · the 21st century
Cardinal labelsNo article when the number is part of a label/name.Room 12 · Page 5 · Chapter 3 · Gate 8 · Flight 204
Specific numbered groupUse the when the number describes a known group.the two guests we met · the three rooms upstairs
Non-specific numberNo article before an ordinary cardinal number.two guests · three temples
Decades / centuriesUse the with decades and named centuries.the 1990s · the 1800s · the 21st century
Hundred / thousand / milliona can mean “one” before hundred/thousand/million.a hundred tourists · a thousand dollars · one million visitors
FractionsUse a with a singular fraction when it means one part.a half · a third · a quarter; but two thirds
DatesWritten styles vary. Spoken ordinals normally take the in “the fourth of September.”September 4 / 4 September · the fourth of September
Historical wars / rulersNumber can be part of a proper name; no normal article before the name.World War II · Henry VIII
Sound rule for a/an: choose by the sound, not the letter: an hour, a university, an MBA, a European.
Common C1 traps: the nature is usually wrong when nature means nature in general; the Bali is wrong; the breakfast is only correct when a specific breakfast is meant; the most people ≠ most people.
