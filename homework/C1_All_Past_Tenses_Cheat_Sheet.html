All Past Tenses — Complete Guide ⏳

Choose the tense by asking one question: What is the speaker focusing on — finished event, background action, earlier result, or earlier process/duration?

📸 Past Simple

V2 / verb + -ed
Use it for
Finished action: It happened and ended in the past.
Story sequence: one event after another.
Past states/facts: something true then.
Helpful words
yesterdaylast weekagoin 2024thensuddenlywhen
Example: The lights went out, the guests screamed, and the detective entered the room.
Negative: did not + base verb → didn't leave
Question: Did + subject + base verb? → Did she leave?

🎥 Past Continuous

was / were + verb-ing
Use it for
Action in progress: already happening at a past moment.
Background: what was happening around the main event.
Interrupted action: longer action + shorter Past Simple event.
Helpful words
whileasat 8 p.m.this time yesterdaywhen + event
Example: The guests were talking when the lights went out.
Negative: was/were not + -ing
Question: Was/Were + subject + -ing?
background action in progressmain event
Classic pair: Past Continuous paints the scene; Past Simple interrupts it or moves the story forward.
Quick decision — page 1
Finished event?One completed action / fact / story step → Past Simple
Already happening?Background or action in progress → Past Continuous
Interrupted?Long action = Continuous; short interrupting event = Simple.
Stative verb?know, believe, want, own usually stay non-continuous.

Earlier Past: Perfect vs Perfect Continuous ⏪

Both look backward from a past point. The difference is the focus: completed result vs process/duration.

✅ Past Perfect

had + V3
Use it for
Earlier completed event: finished before another past action/time.
Result before a past point: quantity or completed outcome matters.
Past experience before then: what had / had not happened before.
Helpful words
beforeafterby the timealreadyjustneveruntil then
Example: By the time the police arrived, the suspect had left.
Negative: had not + V3
Question: Had + subject + V3?

⏳ Past Perfect Continuous

had been + verb-ing
Use it for
Duration before a past point: how long an activity had continued.
Cause of a past result: earlier activity explains evidence you saw then.
Repeated activity: something had been happening again and again.
Helpful words
forsinceall morningfor hourshow longbeforeby the time
Example: His shoes were wet because he had been walking in the rain.
Negative: had not been + -ing
Question: Had + subject + been + -ing?

Result / quantity → Past Perfect

She had written three reports by 9.
Focus = three completed reports.

Process / duration → PPC

She had been writing for three hours by 9.
Focus = activity + duration.
1 · Main finished event?Past Simple.
2 · Background/in progress?Past Continuous.
3 · Earlier + completed/result?Past Perfect.
4 · Earlier + duration/process?Past Perfect Continuous.

Common traps 🚨

✗ She didn't went.
✓ She didn't go.
✗ He was work.
✓ He was working.
✗ They had been finished it.
✓ They had finished it.
✗ She had been knowing him.
✓ She had known him.
✗ I had ate.
✓ I had eaten.
✗ They had been wait.
✓ They had been waiting.
Golden rule: Do not use Past Perfect in every sentence. Use it when you need to make the earlier past clear. Once the timeline is clear, English often returns to Past Simple.
