1 · Quick recap

will: decision now / prediction / promise.
going to: earlier intention or present evidence.
Present Continuous: arrangement.
Future Continuous: in progress at a future time.
Future Perfect: completed by a future time.
Emphasis: clefts, fronting, and inversion.
