🧠 Lesson recap — Grammar

Passive Voice

be + V3

Use the passive when the action/result is more important than the person doing it.

Present: is/are + V3
Past: was/were + V3
Present Perfect: has/have been + V3
Past Perfect: had been + V3

Articles

a/an · the · —

a/an: one non-specific singular noun.
the: specific/known/unique.
—: general plural/uncountable nouns, most cities/countries, meals, by + transport.

Present tenses

Simple · Continuous · Perfect · Perfect Continuous

Habit → Present Simple.
Now → Present Continuous.
Result/experience → Present Perfect.
Duration/process → Present Perfect Continuous.

5. Reading — A New Resort in Ubud 📖

A new eco-resort has recently been opened outside Ubud, in an area that has traditionally been known for rice fields and small family-run guesthouses. The project was designed to use less water and electricity than a conventional hotel, and much of the furniture was made by local craftspeople.

Before construction began, several community meetings had been held so that local residents could discuss the plans. Some people were concerned that the area would become too commercial, while others believed that responsible tourism could create useful jobs.

Since the resort opened, more than half of its staff have been hired from nearby villages. Guests are encouraged to join cultural workshops, but they are also reminded that local traditions should not be treated as entertainment. The management says that new sustainability measures are still being developed and that the resort has been working closely with local organizations.
