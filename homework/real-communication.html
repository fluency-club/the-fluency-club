B1+/B2 Communication Pattern

1. Explain briefly

Give enough context without over-explaining.

2. Acknowledge the inconvenience

Show that you understand how the change affects the other person.

3. Offer a solution

Give one or two realistic alternatives.

4. Confirm the next step

End with a clear question or action.

Something came up, so I can’t make it at six. I know that’s inconvenient. Could we move it to seven, or would tomorrow work better? Keep me posted.
