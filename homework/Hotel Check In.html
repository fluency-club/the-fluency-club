Quick grammar reminder

Present Perfect

have/has + past participle

Use it for a past action connected to now, recent results, unfinished time, and life experience.

The hotel has lost my reservation, so I cannot check in.

Past Perfect

had + past participle

Use it for an action completed before another past action or past reference point.

The hotel had lost my reservation before I arrived.
